01 / Before you start

Install once. Play together.

  1. Use Minecraft Java 1.21.11 with Java 21 and Fabric Loader 0.18.1 or newer.
  2. Install Fabric API for 1.21.11.
  3. Download ProTel, Materials, or both. Put the JAR files in your Minecraft instance’s mods folder.
  4. For multiplayer, install the same mods and Fabric API on the server and every player’s client. Restart the game and server.
Keep only one version of each mod in the mods folder. ProTel 1.2.2 replaces older TELPRO / modtel JARs. Its internal modtel ID is retained for existing items and saves.

These are Fabric builds. They are not NeoForge or Forge builds. ProTel and Materials can currently be installed independently.

02 / ProTel

Your first number.

  1. Get a smartphone and SIM card from Creative, or use the commands below.
  2. Keep the phone in your inventory and press P (default binding).
  3. Open Settings → SIM card and insert your physical SIM.
  4. Choose four digits and register. Your number has the format t1234. Numbers must be unique in the world.
  5. Ask a friend on the same server to register their own SIM. Open Messages or Phone and enter their number.
/give @s modtel:smartphone
/give @s modtel:sim_card
/give @s modtel:sim_card_3g
/give @s modtel:sim_card_5g

Messages & voice

The recipient must be online on the same server and have a phone with an inserted, registered SIM. 3G, 4G and 5G cards all support calls and SMS. A loose SIM in your inventory does not activate the phone.

After a call is accepted, use the microphone button to start talking. The microphone starts muted for each call. Your phone’s volume slider controls call audio and ringtones; Minecraft’s master volume also applies. Calls use the game connection, with no separate voice mod or extra port.

Closing the phone window does not hang up. Removing the SIM, losing the phone, dying or leaving the server ends the call. SMS messages are limited to 1,000 characters; undelivered messages stay as drafts. Offline delivery is not available yet.

Photos, notes & alarms

The camera supports moving the view, selfies and gallery storage. Alarms use real-world time and work while the game is running. Use the Clock app for timers and a stopwatch.

Photos are stored in config/modtel/local/photos. Local phone data is stored in config/modtel/local/phone.json. SIM registration is stored with the world. Back up both when moving installations.

Built-in apps are protected from uninstalling. Use the layout control in quick settings to rearrange apps and move them between pages.

03 / Materials 0.2.2

Find your next vein.

All six materials generate in new Overworld chunks, with stone and deepslate ore variants. Platinum and silicon now sit much closer to the surface.

Changed in 0.2.2: platinum and silicon are limited to Y=−6 through Y=16. Their upper limit is unchanged. Ore origins are inset so the edges of veins stay inside that band.

Mine smarter

  • Start with zinc and bronze. They are common workshop materials, balanced around ordinary underground iron and copper.
  • Try Y=5 for platinum and silicon. Their total frequency remains rare, but their new narrow band makes focused mining worthwhile.
  • Chromium is the rarest. Look deep underground and dig tunnels; exposed cave surfaces hide much of its potential generation.
  • Propylene behaves like deep gold. Aim around Y=−48. It has no badlands bonus.
  • Smelt the ore block. Each ore drops itself and makes one ingot. Fortune does not increase the drop. A furnace takes 10 seconds; a blast furnace takes 5 at 20 TPS.

Rarity comparisons are design targets, not guaranteed counts. Terrain, caves and neighboring veins affect results. Old chunks retain their original ores; explore new terrain to see the new distribution.

Bronze and propylene ores are fictional materials by design.

04 / Production

Press ahead.

Both ProMod presses turn one propylene ingot into one propylene sheet in 100 ticks: 5 seconds at 20 TPS. Right-click a press, place the material in the left slot and collect the output on the right.

Coal press

Your first machine. Insert coal, charcoal or a coal block into the fuel slot.

16sheets per coal
160sheets per coal block

Hoppers: input from above, fuel from the sides, output from below. Fuel is consumed only while processing.

Supply power from a compatible external energy source. The machine accepts Team Reborn Energy API energy from all six sides.

800 Eper sheet
8 E/twhile running

Hoppers: input from above or the sides, output from below. The machine does not output energy.

View crafting →
ProMod does not add a generator or placeable power cables yet. Copper wire is a crafting ingredient. Energy API is bundled with Materials; Fabric API is still a separate requirement.

A full output slot or missing input pauses work without wasting fuel or energy. Machines save their inventories, progress and stored resources. Breaking a machine drops its items; stored energy is lost.

05 / Works with your workshop

Shared materials.
Specific integrations.

Craftings accept common tags such as c:ingots/zinc, c:ingots/platinum, c:ingots/bronze, c:wires/copper and minecraft:planks. Equivalent ores and raw materials can be smelted, too. Older forge tags and the chrome alias are optionally recognized.

The item must carry the right tag; a similar name alone is not enough. Bronze is different from brass. When another mod provides its own smelting recipe, Minecraft may select that mod’s equivalent ingot.

Create Fly pressing

With Create Fly installed, its mechanical press can also turn a propylene ingot into a sheet. Processing speed depends on the Create machine. The recipe is loaded only when Create is present; it was checked with Create Fly 6.0.9-5 for 1.21.11.

Other mods’ machines need their own recipe integrations. Shared energy or material tags alone do not add the sheet recipe to every press.

06 / Good to know

A few quick answers.

Does the server need the mods?

Yes. Install the chosen ProMod JARs and Fabric API on the server and all clients. Single-player LAN also works when every participant has the required mods.

Why can’t I find the new ores?

Explore newly generated Overworld chunks. Existing chunks are not regenerated. Check the Y ranges above and make sure the server is running Materials 0.2.2.

Why does my call have no sound?

Accept the call, enable the in-call microphone on the speaking side, then check phone and Minecraft volume. ProTel uses your system’s default audio devices. Headphones help avoid echo. Voice packets were tested with synthetic audio; real microphones and drivers can behave differently.

Why is my phone out of service?

Insert a registered SIM into the phone through Settings. Keep the phone in your inventory. An unregistered or loose SIM will not provide service.

Is there an app store or internet?

Not in these releases. Internet, an app store, Wi-Fi infrastructure and Bluetooth devices are planned for future cooperating mods. Their interface elements do not currently provide those services.

Why does my electric press do nothing?

Provide a compatible external energy source, a propylene ingot and space in the output slot. A copper wire item cannot transmit power. Try the coal press if you have no compatible generator.

Can I craft the phone or SIM?

Not yet. Use Creative or the commands in the phone setup section. The recipe book covers the recipes that exist today.

Where should I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub with the Minecraft version, mod versions, steps to reproduce and relevant log lines. Remove server addresses or personal information you do not want to share.
