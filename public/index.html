ProTel / v1.2.2

A little closer.
Even worlds apart.

A real number, a physical SIM card and your friends on the same server.

Set up your first phone →

Calls & messages

Register a number such as t1234. Exchange SMS and start voice calls with online players, even across dimensions.

Make it yours

Choose a theme and font, arrange your app icons and use multiple home-screen pages. The phone follows your Minecraft language.

Capture the moment

Use the camera, switch to selfie mode and keep your pictures in the gallery. Photos are saved locally.

Everyday essentials

Notes, contacts, a calculator, real-time alarms, a stopwatch and a timer. Quick settings and volume controls are a swipe away.

Materials / v0.2.2

Start with a vein.
Build a workshop.

Zinc, bronze, chromium, platinum, silicon and propylene. A distinct resource for every next step.

Open the recipe book →

Six materials

Stone and deepslate variants, with one smelted material per ore block. Bronze and propylene are intentionally fictional ores.

Two ways to press

Use coal for your first machine, or supply compatible energy to an electric press. Both turn propylene into sheets.

Shared ingredients

Recipes accept correctly tagged equivalent materials from other mods, including different kinds of planks.

Shallower discoveries

In v0.2.2, platinum and silicon generate between Y=−6 and Y=16. Chromium remains the rarest material, deep underground.

New ores appear in newly generated Overworld chunks. Existing terrain is left untouched.
