PRO MOD MATERIALS 0.5.0 — RECIPE REFERENCE
Minecraft Java 1.21.11 / Fabric

COPPER WIRE (shapeless, 2x2 or 3x3)
1 copper ingot + 1 pair of shears -> 2 copper wires.
Shears stay in the grid, lose 1 durability, and break when exhausted.
Unbreakable shears do not lose durability.

ELECTRIC PRESS (3x3)
wire   | zinc     | wire
iron   | magnetite | iron
planks | iron     | planks
2 wires, 1 zinc ingot, 1 magnetite ingot, 3 iron ingots, 2 planks.

COAL PRESS (3x3)
wire       | lead | wire
coal block | empty  | coal block
planks     | planks | planks
2 wires, 1 lead ingot, 2 coal blocks, 3 planks.

TUNGSTEN PLATE (pressing)
1 tungsten ingot -> 1 tungsten plate.
Coal press: 100 ticks / 5 seconds. Electric press: 20 ticks / 1 second.
Times assume 20 TPS.
Electric cost: 800 E, 40 E per tick. Buffer: 16,000 E.
Coal or charcoal: 16 plates. Coal block: 160 plates.
Optional Create Fly mechanical press recipe; speed depends on Create.

SMELTING (all six materials, both ore variants)
Zinc -> zinc ingot; lead -> lead ingot; chromite -> chromium ingot;
magnetite -> magnetite ingot; Viner Quartz -> Viner Quartz crystal;
wolframite -> tungsten ingot. All recipes: 1 ore block -> 1 product.
Furnace: 200 ticks / 10 seconds. Blast furnace: 100 ticks / 5 seconds.
Both need fuel. Ore drops itself; Fortune does not multiply the drop.

COMMON INGREDIENTS
Recipes use c:ingots/*, c:wires/copper, c:storage_blocks/coal and
minecraft:planks. Different planks can be mixed. Correctly tagged ores and
raw materials from other mods can also be smelted. Lead is not brass.

PHONE / SIM
No phone or SIM crafting recipes exist yet. Use Creative or commands:
/give @s modtel:smartphone
/give @s modtel:sim_card

RECIPE VIEWERS (OPTIONAL)
JEI 27.4.0.16 or REI 21.11.816 for Minecraft 1.21.11 Fabric.
REI additionally needs Architectury 19.0.1 and Cloth Config 21.11.153.
Coal pressing, electric pressing and wire cutting have dedicated categories.
Press recipes synchronize from the server, including datapack recipes.
Install one viewer on the client; ProMod Materials and Fabric API belong
on both the server and clients. Viewers are not bundled or required.

Shared tags work in both directions: modern c:, legacy forge: and flat
Fabric tags, including chrome/chromium and c:silicon aliases. Materials
must have the matching tag; similarly named items are not enough.
Lead, bronze and brass are separate materials. Viner Quartz is not Nether quartz.


TIN / MIXING SMELTERS — 0.3.1
1 zinc ingot + 1 lead ingot + 1 copper ingot = 1 tin ingot.
Place ingredients in any input slots. Common c:ingots material tags apply.
Coal: 6 inputs, 1 output, 1 fuel slot; 10 seconds (200 ticks) per batch.
Electric: 9 inputs, 1 output, 8000 mB fluid tank; 5 seconds (100 ticks).
Electric cost: 4000 E at 40 E/t; 32000 E buffer, up to 256 E/t input.
Tin requires no fluid. The electric tank can be filled and drained by
Fabric Transfer API pipes on all sides, or compatible fluid containers.
It stores one fluid variant. Create Fly 6.0.9-5 fluid bridge was checked.
Machine crafting is available below. Creative /give is also supported:
/give @s promod-materials:coal_mixing_smelter
/give @s promod-materials:electric_mixing_smelter
JEI and REI show both mixing categories. Tin has no ProMod ore.


CLEARED SILICON / UV SOLUTION — 0.4.0
Shapeless crafting, 2x2 or 3x3:
1 milk bucket + 1 water bucket + 1 copper ingot + 1 zinc ingot
-> 1 UV Solution bucket (1000 mB) + 1 empty bucket.
The water bucket becomes the UV bucket; the milk bucket is returned empty.

Electric Mixing Smelter only:
1 Viner Quartz crystal + 1 Nether quartz + 1 tin ingot + 1 magnetite ingot
+ 1000 mB UV Solution from the tank -> 1 Cleared Silicon.
100 ticks / 5 seconds at 20 TPS; 4000 E. Fluid consumed at completion.
Fill the tank with a bucket or compatible pipes, not the item input slots.
Full output or insufficient UV pauses processing without wasting resources.
Shared tags: c:gems/viner_quartz, c:gems/quartz, c:ingots/tin,
c:ingots/magnetite, c:ingots/copper, c:ingots/zinc.
UV Solution cannot form infinite sources. Tin still requires no fluid.
JEI and REI show UV crafting and the fluid requirement for Cleared Silicon.


MIXING SMELTER CRAFTING — 0.5.0
Coal Mixing Smelter, 3x3, produces 1 machine:
coal block | coal block | coal block
plate      | zinc       | diamond
planks     | planks     | planks
1 tungsten plate, 3 planks, 3 coal blocks, 1 diamond, 1 zinc ingot.

Electric Mixing Smelter, 3x3, produces 1 machine:
iron block | iron block | iron block
plate      | diamond    | plate
tin        | diamond    | tin
3 iron blocks, 2 tungsten plates, 2 diamonds, 2 tin ingots.

Different planks can be mixed. Equivalent materials from other mods are
accepted through minecraft:planks, c:storage_blocks/coal,
c:storage_blocks/iron, c:plates/tungsten, c:gems/diamond,
c:ingots/zinc and c:ingots/tin. Plates also accept c:plates/tungsten.
Recipes unlock in the recipe book when you obtain a plate.
Both presses have refreshed original pixel textures and corrected panel UVs;
their processing times remain 5 seconds (coal) and 1 second (electric).

RESERVED MATERIALS / MIGRATION — 0.5.0
Platinum and silicon ingots remain as separate Creative items for future
recipes, with no ores or production recipes. Old renamed ore blocks and
item stacks migrate when loaded. Existing platinum/silicon ingots stay intact.
