Three steps

From download to first launch.

01. Match your version

Use Minecraft Java 1.21.11, Java 21 and Fabric Loader 0.18.1 or newer.

02. Add the files

Put your ProMod JARs and matching Fabric API in the instance’s mods folder.

03. Bring your friends

For multiplayer, install the same files on the server and every player’s client.

Release notes

What’s changed.

MATERIALS 0.4.1
07 NOV 2026

Build your mixing line.

Both Mixing Smelters now have survival crafting recipes, using common tags for modded ingredients and mixed wood types. Presses receive new pixel textures, steel pistons, clearer controls and corrected panel UVs. Coal uses warm metal and a firebox; electric uses ivory and teal. Recipe-book unlocks, JEI and REI support are included.

MATERIALS 0.4.0
07 NOV 2026

A clearer next step.

Craft UV Solution from milk, water, copper and zinc, then combine silicon, quartz, tin and platinum with 1000 mB of solution to make Cleared Silicon. The electric Mixing Smelter consumes exactly one bucket per 5-second cycle. Includes original item textures, a violet fluid, compatible pipe handling, shared material tags and JEI / REI recipe views.

MATERIALS 0.3.1
05 NOV 2026

A workshop with character.

Redesigned mixing smelters feature open vats, visible mixing shafts and paddles, and distinct coal and electric machinery. Coal uses warm metal bands, a firebox and a chimney; electric uses ivory and teal panels, a control display and a side tank. Website previews now use the actual model geometry and pixel textures. Recipes and processing times are unchanged.

MATERIALS 0.3.0
05 NOV 2026

Mix your next material.

Combine one zinc, one bronze and one copper ingot into one tin ingot. The coal mixing smelter offers 6 input slots and a 10-second cycle; the electric version has 9 inputs, a 5-second cycle and an 8-bucket fluid tank with pipe support. Both have one output slot. JEI and REI show the new process. Machine crafting recipes will follow; use Creative or commands for now.

MATERIALS 0.2.4
02 NOV 2026

A better-connected workshop.

Optional JEI and REI categories show both presses and copper wire cutting, including server-synchronized datapack recipes. Expanded shared tags let equivalent materials work across mods, with legacy Fabric and forge tags, chrome/chromium aliases and shared silicon support.

MATERIALS 0.2.3
01 NOV 2026

Electric pressing, five times faster.

The electric press now makes a sheet in 1 second (20 ticks). The coal press takes 5 seconds (100 ticks). Electric energy cost stays at 800 E per sheet, with a draw of 40 E/t. Progress bars and translated machine labels follow the correct speed.

MATERIALS 0.2.2
01 NOV 2026

Rare minerals, closer to the surface.

Platinum and silicon now generate from Y=−6 to Y=16. Their upper limit is unchanged, and the other four materials keep their existing distributions. Applies to newly generated chunks.

MATERIALS 0.2.1

A better-balanced underground.

Chromium became the rarest material. Zinc and bronze became common workshop resources; propylene moved deep underground.

MATERIALS 0.2.0

Your first production line.

Copper wire, propylene sheets, coal and electric presses, shared ingredient tags and optional Create Fly pressing support.

PROTEL 1.2.2

Stay in touch.

Registered physical SIM cards, player-to-player SMS and voice calls. ProTel retains the internal modtel ID for existing data. Internet and the app store remain future features.
